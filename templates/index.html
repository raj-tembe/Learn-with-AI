RETRIEVAL-AUGMENTED GENERATION (RAG)

A real retrieval pipeline, not a keyword search.

Every answer is mathematically proven against your uploaded documents. If your files do not contain the answer, Learn with AI tells you directly instead of speculating.

01

Multi-Modal Document Ingestion

Drag and drop PDFs, text notes, CSV datasets, JSON data, or online Wikipedia articles. Files are chunked recursively and converted into high-dimensional semantic vectors via sentence-transformers.

Isolated ChromaDB Store
02

Cross-Encoder Re-Ranking

Standard keyword search returns noisy matches. Our two-stage pipeline uses an ms-marco cross-encoder model to score and re-rank the top candidates, discarding irrelevant paragraphs before synthesis.

Top-3 Precision Scoring
03

Tone-Calibrated Synthesis

Google Gemini synthesizes the exact answers constrained strictly to the re-ranked excerpts, tailored precisely to your chosen explanatory tone and difficulty level with citations.

Strict Fact-Grounding
